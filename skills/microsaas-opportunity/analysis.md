# Analysis

Output labels stay Chinese; product names stay in their original language.

## Select (list)

Re-rank the gathered posts by MicroSaaS fit. Pick **up to 4** a solo founder could ship, operate, and market. Prefer: clear paying audience, a credible v1 in weeks/months, no 24/7 ops team.

Leave out of the 4:

- Hardware / physical products
- Enterprise B2B that needs a sales team or a compliance program
- Pure entertainment that depends on viral consumer marketing
- Products that need 24/7 ops, heavy infra, or a moderation team
- Native-mobile-only with no indie monetization path
- Heavily VC-funded launches in a saturated, defended market

If fewer than 4 fit, use the fit set and say the shortfall. If none fit, skip the deep-dive section.

**Product branch:** skip this list as a filter — still fill 描述 and all five parts; a poor fit shows up in the ⭐ row.

## 描述

2–4 complete Chinese sentences per **selected** product, same depth for every item in the report. Ground in gathered fields only (`tagline`, `description`, `topics`, vote/comment counts, or the fetched page). Missing fields are named gaps inside the 描述 (e.g. 官方简介极短，以下主要从标语与话题归纳).

- **Sentence 1–2:** who it is for and what job it does, from `tagline` / `description` (or the page one-liner). Translate faithfully.
- **Sentence 3–4:** positioning from `topics` and scale (votes/comments or pricing/audience on the page). No feature names, integrations, or roadmap items that are not in the gathered fields.

## Five parts

Analytical voice. The 描述 sits in the product block above these parts; do not restate it here.

### 切入角度

One stance, and why it still has room (the PH/web product is already shipping):

| Stance | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| 复刻 | commodity tech; a solo can rebuild quickly |
| 垂直化 | same job, narrower vertical |
| 平价替代 | same value at a fraction of the price by stripping enterprise |
| 配套工具 | orthogonal complement (importer, analytics, integration) |
| 去 SaaS 化 | local-first / one-time purchase / open-source alternative |

### 竞品与市场验证

2–4 existing competitors. Validated / saturated / nascent. PH vote count is one demand signal when present (e.g. 1,000+ on a day).

### MicroSaaS 适配度

One ⭐ row (`⭐⭐⭐⭐☆`). Never "4/5" or "4 stars".

| Dimension | What to weigh |
|-----------|----------------|
| 市场需求与变现 | paying audience, path to revenue |
| 复刻 / 切入门槛 | solo v1 in weeks/months |
| 运营和维护 | infra, 24/7 ops, moderation, compliance |
| 竞争壁垒 | niche vs race-to-zero |
| 产品化 + 推广 | one person can ship and market |

Low scores stay low when the track is crowded or ops-heavy.

### 短评

One or two sentences that explain the ⭐ row.

### 差异化建议

One concrete wedge a solo could ship in 2–8 weeks: name the niche, the missing feature, the pricing, or the channel — a sentence that could go on a one-pager tomorrow.

3–5 sentences, one paragraph, from **this run's** gathered posts and selected deep dives: shared categories, crowded tracks, adjacent niches the day hints at.
